SMU Study — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 8, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the SMU Study Android application ("the app", "we") collects, uses, and protects information from study participants.

The study is conducted by researchers at the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health. The Principal Investigator is Dr. Yuning Liu (yuning_liu@g.harvard.edu). Day-to-day study contact: Darwin Li (darwinli@college.harvard.edu).

The study has been submitted for review by the Harvard University-Area Institutional Review Board (IRB) and is pending approval. The app is not used to enroll participants until IRB approval is granted. Once approved, the IRB protocol number will be added to this policy.

1. Who can use this app

The SMU Study app is invitation-only. It is distributed via the Prolific research participant platform to adults age 18 or older who have signed up for the study and provided informed consent. The app is not intended for, and will not knowingly collect data from, anyone under the age of 18.

2. What information we collect

While the app is in active use during the 7-day study window, we collect only the data items below. We collect nothing before the participant completes onboarding and gives consent, and nothing after the 7 days end.

  • Prolific Participant ID — a 24-character anonymous code assigned by Prolific. This is the only identifier we keep; we do not collect your real name, email, or phone number.
  • App session events — for the social media apps you select during onboarding (from a fixed list of Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Snapchat, X/Twitter, WhatsApp, and Discord), we record the package name, the time you opened the app, the time you closed it, and the study day number.
  • In-the-moment survey responses — when you open one of your selected apps during the sampling window you chose, the app may show a brief one-question survey. We record your answer, the timestamp, and which app it was associated with. Maximum 15 such surveys per day per the study protocol.
  • End-of-day (EMA) survey responses — at the two times each day you scheduled (default 5pm and 9pm), the app sends a notification asking you to complete a short well-being survey. We record your answers and timestamps.
  • Study group assignment — at enrollment you are randomly assigned to one of two study conditions. We store this assignment.

3. What we explicitly do NOT collect

  • We do not read or record screen content, keystrokes, photos, contacts, calendar, location, microphone audio, or camera.
  • We do not collect any data from apps you did not select during onboarding.
  • We do not collect data outside your chosen sampling window or after the 7-day study period.
  • We do not access browsing history or any data inside the social media apps themselves.

4. Why the app needs Accessibility and Display-over-other-apps permissions

The app uses Android's Accessibility Service API for the single, narrow purpose of detecting when a participant opens one of the social media apps they selected during onboarding. The service responds only to system "window state changed" events to read the foreground package name; it does not read screen contents, intercept input, or interact with other apps in any way.

The app uses the Display-over-other-apps permission to render the brief one-question in-the-moment survey on top of the social media app at the moment of opening, since this is the only reliable way on Android 13+ to display a time-sensitive prompt at the moment of app launch.

Both permissions are used only for the research purposes described above. Both can be revoked at any time in your phone's Settings.

5. How your information is used

Data collected through the app is used only for the academic research study you consented to. Specifically: to evaluate whether brief in-the-moment motivation reflection prompts during social media use are associated with changes in well-being outcomes over the 7-day study window.

6. How and where data is stored

Data is transmitted from your device over an encrypted (HTTPS / TLS) connection to a secure backend hosted on Amazon Web Services in the United States (US East region). Access to the backend is restricted to the study research team. The data is not sold, rented, traded, or shared with advertisers, marketers, or any third party for commercial purposes.

Anonymized or aggregated results may be published in academic journals, conference proceedings, or theses. No individual participant will be identifiable in any published output.

7. Retention

Identifiable study data (linked to your Prolific ID) is retained for seven (7) years following the conclusion of the study, in accordance with Harvard's standard human-subjects data retention policy. After this period, the data is either de-identified for ongoing scientific use or securely destroyed, per the IRB-approved retention plan.

8. Your rights

You may at any time:

  • Withdraw from the study by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling stops all further data collection immediately.
  • Request deletion of all data we have collected from you, by emailing darwinli@college.harvard.edu with your Prolific ID. We will delete the records within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • Request a copy of the data we have collected from you, using the same email address.

Withdrawal or deletion does not affect any compensation already earned through Prolific for participation completed up to that point.

9. Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect your data, including TLS encryption in transit, restricted-access cloud storage, and strict access controls limiting backend access to authorized members of the research team. No system is perfectly secure, but the dataset contains no real-world identifiers that could be linked to you outside the Prolific platform.

10. Changes to this policy

If we materially change how we collect or use participant data, we will update this policy and notify active participants via in-app notification or Prolific message before the change takes effect.
